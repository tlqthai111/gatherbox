    What would happen next in a launched store

    These steps describe the proposed process. None of them happen in this prototype.

    1. The customer receives an order confirmation email.
    2. The team picks and packs the kit, with a proposed target of dispatch within two business days. This is not verified performance or a delivery guarantee.
    3. The carrier delivers the order and the customer can follow its tracking status.
