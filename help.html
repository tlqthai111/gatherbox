Frequently asked questions

About the kits

What is in each kit?

Every kit contains plates, cups, forks and napkins in equal numbers, all in one neutral style.

  • 10-guest kit: 10 plates, 10 cups, 10 forks and 10 napkins.
  • 20-guest kit: 20 plates, 20 cups, 20 forks and 20 napkins.

Kits do not include knives, spoons, serving ware or decorations.

Which kit should I choose?

Choose the 10-guest kit for up to 10 people and the 20-guest kit for up to 20. For larger groups, combine kits: for example, 30 guests would need one 20-guest kit and one 10-guest kit. See the full guide on the shop page.

What are the plates, cups, forks and napkins made of?

This has not been confirmed yet. GatherBox has not selected a supplier, so we do not make any claims about materials, recyclability or compostability. Materials would be listed on each kit once confirmed.

Are there other colours or themes?

No. GatherBox plans to offer one neutral style so that kits suit most occasions and are simple to choose.

Delivery and returns Proposed

The answers in this group describe a proposed policy. It is not in effect.

Where would GatherBox deliver?

The proposed delivery area is the Sydney metropolitan area. Exact suburbs and postcodes have not been confirmed.

How much would delivery cost?

This prototype uses one sample flat delivery charge of $12.00 per order. A real charge would depend on carrier quotes, which have not been obtained.

How long would delivery take?

The proposed target is to dispatch paid orders within two business days. Delivery times after dispatch would depend on the carrier and are not confirmed. This is a proposed business target, not verified performance or a delivery guarantee.

What would the returns policy be?

Proposed arrangements for missing or damaged items, returns and refunds remain to be finalised before any launch. No return or reporting deadlines have been agreed.

About this prototype

Is GatherBox a real store?

No. GatherBox is a prototype created for a UTS student assessment. It is not a registered or trading business. Meet the team.

How is payment handled?

In this prototype, payment is only simulated: at checkout you choose whether the payment is approved or declined. No card details are requested. A launched store would use a hosted payment provider, so GatherBox would not store card details itself.
