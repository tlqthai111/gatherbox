Tableware kits for Sydney gatherings

Count your guests. Choose one kit. Set the table.

GatherBox is a proposed online store for Sydney households arranging birthdays, picnics and casual get-togethers. Each kit contains matching plates, cups, forks and napkins for 10 or 20 guests, so the essentials arrive together in one order.

Made for small, casual occasions

GatherBox is planned for people hosting at home or nearby, rather than for caterers or large events.

  • Birthdays

    Set the table for a family birthday without buying plates, cups, forks and napkins separately.

  • Picnics

    Pack one kit for the park and know exactly how many place settings you are bringing.

  • Casual gatherings

    For barbecues, games nights and catch-ups where you want the table sorted quickly.

How ordering works

The store is designed so you can see exactly what you are buying and what it costs before you pay.

  1. Count your guests

    Up to 10 guests? Choose the 10-guest kit. Up to 20? Choose the 20-guest kit. More? Combine kits.

  2. Check what is inside

    Every kit lists its exact contents and price before you add it to your cart.

  3. See the full cost

    Your cart shows the kit prices, the delivery charge and the total before checkout.

  4. Order once

    Everything is paid for in one order. In this prototype, payment is only simulated.

What is still being confirmed

GatherBox is at the planning stage. Before any launch, the team would need to confirm:

  • the materials used for plates, cups, forks and napkins, and who supplies them
  • final prices and the delivery charge
  • delivery partners, delivery times and the exact Sydney delivery area.
