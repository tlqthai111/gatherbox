About this prototype

This site is student assessment work. It is not an official UTS website, and GatherBox is not a registered or trading business.

The prototype demonstrates a business-to-consumer ordering journey:

  • comparing two kit sizes and their exact contents
  • reviewing a cart with a delivery charge and total
  • a checkout with a simulated payment result
  • a demonstration order confirmation.

It does not take payments, request card details, store orders on a server or deliver anything. Prices and the delivery charge are sample values.
